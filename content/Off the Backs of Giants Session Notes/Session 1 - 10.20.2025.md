Session Notes

***August 6, 3277 AC - The Dragon's Rest Festival***

The night of the [[Dragon's Rest Festival]] was well under way as the party made their way through the crowded and lively streets of [[Iulia]] to the [[Hall of Heroes]]. Late to the proceedings going on in the historic location, they arrived in time to partake in the festivities and converse with some of colorful people who were there to join the legions of adventurers looking to lend their skills and train under the seasoned veterans of the Great City.

[[Varan]] pulled [[Isadora]] aside to comfort her since she was feeling overwhelmed by it all and in their isolation they were approached by a large, slightly unsettling man who called himself [[Sven Beck]] and expressed interest in the relics the two flaunted, suggesting they help each other going forward since they common interests. 

Making his way to the wine, [[Erlyn MacNamara]] went to dunk his hand into the bowl before being stopped by [[an unkown half-orc woman]] who asked him not to do that. Not to be outdone, he followed her directions and got a glass, only to dunk the entire thing into the bowl along with his hand and drank his now-contaminated wine, much to her dismay. 

Eventually, he would be approached by the impressively tall [[Kalina]] who would exchange some quick words with him before convincing him to join her on the dancefloor. There, guard down slightly from the drink perhaps, he told her a bit about his criminal past, though she didn't seem to take the matter to heart.

Looking to get some food, [[Isadora]] and [[Varan]] joined by [[Sven Beck|Sven]] made their way towards the food stations, though [[Isadora]] ran into a cloaked man, nearly spilling his drink. He turned and apologized despite not being at fault, introducing himself as [[Masoud]]. 

After some small talk and witnessing [[Erlyn MacNamara|Erlyn]], frankly, making a fool of themselves, the small group that was forming was approached by both [[Iola]] and [[Rolando Cuocco]] who joined them in their conversation.

Proceedings came to a pause as the room was addressed and [[Archduke Lorien Duborsia]], accompanied by a group of guards, his personal bodyguard, his grandfather [[Gluvianus Duborsia]] wished the room a happy [[Dragon's Rest Festival]] and gave a speech reminding them of the city's legacy of heroes who have changed the course of history expressing his category that they would choose to train and lend their efforts to the city.

Once the speech came to a close, [[Varan]], encouraged by [[Rolando Cuocco|Rolando]], got decently drunk as [[Archduke Lorien Duborsia|the archduke]] greeted the party's guest, asking some questions about the city's history but concluding that perhaps he wasn't the one to ask due to his perhaps disingenuous demeanor. After a bit more small talk, the large group split and went down to bed for the evening in separate provided rooms. 

[[Varan]] would not be so lucky as to get a full night of sleep, experiencing a strange nightmare that saw him working with his fellow lizardfolk back in his homeland of [[Dartmoor]]. Helping his clanmates, they pulled up a fishing net from the shallows, though it contained no fish. Instead, all that it held was the khopesh relic that he came across before making his way to [[Iulia]]. His waking memories returning to him in the moment, the dreamscape burned away and left a tangible nothing. One that left him felt watched. Stalked. From it, before he could react, something attacked him, thrusting him back into the waking world.

As he collected himself, reaching for his blade for security, he heard that while the festivities seemed to have died down, there was still activity. Banging, shouting, and loud steps. As he got up to approach the door, somebody bashed it down in one swoop and crashed to the ground, a crazed look in his eyes.

Now awake, the party armed themselves and defended themselves from a group of ruffians, seemingly in a drug-induced rage. Across the hall, [[Sven Beck|Sven]] came under attack as well and attempted to lend a hand in the combat, though failed to accomplish much between his weariness, fresh head trauma, and frenzied state of mind. Still, even if with some difficulty, the group managed to neutralize their aggressors and have properly armed themselves, getting ready to investigate the matter further.
